# 0007 — Retained message budgets and quarantine ownership

Status: logical controls implemented and verified; physical-volume pressure remains an operations-verification gate. Measured results are in the progress ledger.

Unpublished event capacity and retained replay history are different budgets. Each append now reserves both counters in its owner's transaction. Publication releases only the unpublished reservation. Retained outbox payload is capped at 256 MiB per owner; new intake and dispatch stop at 80% of this limit or the 512 MiB owner database budget, leaving headroom for already accepted outcomes. The database measurement includes indexes and allocated relation files. It is not a filesystem free-space measurement or a hard PVC quota. [PostgreSQL documents `pg_database_size` and its CONNECT privilege requirement](https://www.postgresql.org/docs/18/functions-admin.html).

The runtime checks only its own database, using its existing restricted connection. Database and message pressure remain readable through platform diagnostics. Physical host/PVC free-space guarding, measured disk exhaustion and recovery remain explicit operations-verification work; these logical limits alone do not establish A26.

Every 30 seconds, bounded retention transactions inspect at most 100 rows of each eligible category. Published outbox payloads remain for at least seven days after both creation and publication. Pending, paused and leased publication records are retained. Applied inbox payloads can be compacted only after both receipt and application are older than seven days. Inbox event IDs, payload hashes, source identities, stream cursors and semantic version tombstones remain. A later duplicate still has no second effect, and contradictory contents still quarantine. This bounds payload history without deleting accepted business records. Backups older than the supported replay horizon are not silently treated as recoverable.

Original malformed or untrusted delivery bytes stay in a separate durable table. A site is attached only when its claim agrees with an authenticated allowed publisher exchange and the configured subscription. Site-less entries expose only opaque diagnostic IDs, hashes, sizes, reason codes and times to platform administrators. No payload, transport ID or claimed business identity is exposed through this global diagnostic view. Ordinary site lists exclude this pool.

A supervisor reprocesses a site-qualified entry with an expected version, idempotency key and correction reason. The exact original bytes, publisher exchange and transport identity run through the current contract/source/site checks. A configuration or consumer correction may permit transfer to the durable inbox; reprocessing itself never edits a payload or authorizes another site. Successful transfer, quota changes and audit commit together. Unchanged invalid data remains quarantined. Transferred raw payloads can be compacted after the same seven-day horizon; unresolved originals are retained.

All retention, manual message recovery and post-network task writes honor the shared worker-freeze gate. Pausing intake alone would not freeze a checkpoint. Replayed requests that already have a recorded response remain readable without reopening a mutation.
