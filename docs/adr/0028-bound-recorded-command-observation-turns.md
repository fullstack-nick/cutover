# ADR 0028: bound recorded-command observation turns

Date: 9 September 2026. Status: implemented; all 12 execution workflow checks and the complete 202-check repository verification passed; deployed causal checks passed, sustained latency qualification failed.

The full `load-1788918305739` missed the unchanged dispatch target after the approved cache experiment. A subsequent short instrumented diagnostic, `load-diagnostic-1788919918808`, reproduced the delay: 81.667% of 600 measured movements within two seconds, p99 10,407.935 ms, with 650 single effects and zero database deadlocks. Its 149 database/observation samples and four sets of worker dumps captured WAL waits while equipment observations remained fresh, with maximum sampled age 1.243439 seconds. Execution CPU throttling was zero between the first and last captured stalls; this limited interval does not establish all-run CPU behavior. The final captured execution stack was waiting for a recorded-command observation's database commit in `contextSnapshot`, before processing unrecorded candidates in that group.

[ADR 0023](0023-fair-scheduler-work-selection.md) reserved fresh candidate slots, but it still allowed an observation-only claim to borrow all sixteen places. Inside a mixed group, status observations ran before new dispatch. A slow sequence of already-recorded command checks could therefore delay both work selected in the same batch and work arriving during an observation-only turn.

The execution coordinator now selects at most twelve fresh candidates and four retry/observation candidates. The second group stays bounded at four even when the first group is empty. After claiming under the existing leases, it processes work without a local dispatch-acceptance timestamp across all selected zones before processing recorded-command observations. Unknown outcomes still require adapter evidence under the original command identity. Completion events remain immediate; fallback still receives a turn, and next-attempt ordering rotates that bounded share.

The change adds no threads, shared task model or new authority. Proposal rules, owner/epoch checks, freshness, transport retry limits, transaction durability and physical reconciliation remain unchanged. The tradeoff is lower fallback catch-up capacity during observation-only turns, in exchange for a smaller amount of status work ahead of newly arriving requests. Legacy and returns retain their separate coordinator implementations; this change addresses the captured execution-service path.

Two regressions use actual core, execution, adapter and simulator databases with controlled time and transport ports. `slowRecordedObservationsFollowNewDispatch` delays a recorded command's context while fresh work is already waiting. `newArrivalWaitsForOnlyABoundedObservationTurn` introduces fresh work during an observation-only turn and applies a per-command observation delay. Both failed against the previous scheduler at 04:18:11 and 04:21:53 Europe/Berlin respectively. They require timely fresh dispatch while retaining the original recorded commands, single effects and a bounded observation turn. Their controlled timings are component evidence; a passing full ten-minute workload remains necessary to qualify A50.

The full execution workflow class passed all 12 checks at 04:24:00 Europe/Berlin after the correction, with zero failures, errors or skips. Complete offline repository verification of `6e833d1` then passed **202 checks across 25 classes** at **04:34:25** in **541 seconds**, with zero failures, errors or skips. [Backend evidence](../evidence/backend-checks.json) records the source and original log hash.

Images `beeabc1` preserved the existing warehouse world and routes through rollout; `causal-trace-1788921706328` passed both causal checks. The full `load-1788921802696` subsequently failed A50 at **04:55:00 Europe/Berlin**: **89.6%** of all 3,000 measured movements within two seconds, original-eligibility p99 **11,364.91 ms**. All 1,980 requests returned 202, all 3,300 effects completed once and all six database deadlock deltas were zero. The bounded observation turn fixes the demonstrated ordering defect but is insufficient to satisfy sustained latency. The [load evidence](../evidence/healthy-load.md) retains the complete unchanged workload and resource measurements.
