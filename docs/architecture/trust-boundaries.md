# Trust boundaries and recovery risks

This local lab protects synthetic business state and simulated physical effects. It assumes the machine owner controls Docker, the source checkout and the private configuration directory. A host administrator can replace code or inspect process memory; these controls do not defend against that administrator.

| Entry or failure | Protected property | Implemented controls | Remaining boundary |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Browser and direct HTTP callers | Role/site authority and valid identity | Standard JWT signature, issuer, audience and time validation; server-side site predicates; explicit supervisor actions; PKCE and memory-only browser tokens | Browser origin is loopback HTTP, not end-to-end browser TLS. |
| Generic or shadow service credential | Restricted client cannot originate unauthorized work | Explicit intake client allowlist; shadow denial even with broad service roles; independent adapter identity fences and Calico policy | Compromise of an admitted intake client can submit bounded synthetic work for its allowed site. |
| Forged completion event | Inventory and task state reflect authorized equipment evidence | Restricted publisher exchanges; receiver source/exchange checks; schema/site/identity validation; immutable command evidence | A broker administrator belongs to the local platform trust boundary. |
| Duplicate or reordered transport | One business effect and recoverable original evidence | Durable inbox identities, aggregate sequence handling, independent business uniqueness, raw-payload quarantine | Delivery is at least once; retries can repeat transport. |
| Raw delivery recovery | Transfer is acknowledged only to actual durable inbox ownership | Typed admitted/quarantined receipts; no inference from equality between separate UUID namespaces; retained byte accounting | Replay cannot repair arbitrary invalid bytes; genuine source/contract repair is required. |
| Storage closure with fresh request keys | New metadata cannot bypass admission | Admission precedes fresh-key business-duplicate responses; recorded keys remain readable; actual owner and volume high-water checks | Local disk and database availability still bound durability. |
| Stale owner after migration | Only one active command authority per zone | Durable allocation, owner/epoch validation under route locks, finite drain and reconciled proof before switch | Manual database edits by the machine owner can violate the model and are outside supported operation. |
| Equipment response lost or history changed | No guessed resend or second physical movement | Stable immutable command ID; separate acceptance/execution ledger; same-world/generation history proof; versioned supervisor investigation | Missing physical/business history stays blocked or quarantined. |
| Stale application restoration | Restored intent cannot rewind physical work | Separate restore storage, frozen checkpoint, original-event replay, dispatch disabled until reconciliation, explicit RPO gap | Same-host backups do not protect against host-disk loss. |
| Secret/configuration exposure | Public source and observations contain no credentials | Generated credentials in ignored storage; owner/SYSTEM local permissions; secret mounts, sanitized telemetry, public-source review | Host administrator and deliberately started local management tools can access their authorized secrets. |
| Monitoring or broker failure | Observations do not become business authority | Bounded trace/export queues; process-local liveness; retained outbox and controlled intake rejection | Monitoring absence is visible uncertainty, not evidence of a healthy zero. |

The completed static review at `9ee083b` covered 430 tracked files and found three low-severity local/authenticated issues. [ADR 0020](../adr/0020-intake-and-recovery-capabilities.md) records their corrections. The immutable raw scan remains private. Regression checks cover the revised boundaries; actual deployed checks and their limits are recorded in the [implementation ledger](../planning/implementation-progress.md). No blanket claim of production security or complete tenant isolation follows from this review.

Keep [authentication](../runbooks/identity-outage.md), [message recovery](../operations/message-quarantine.md), [command investigation](../operations/command-investigation.md) and [restoration](../runbooks/restore.md) procedures tied to their specific authorities. A platform administrator's configuration role is separate from a supervisor's business recovery role.
